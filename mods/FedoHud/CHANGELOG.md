# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- New mod, built for Valheim 1.0. Draggable in-game clock, following the day/night
  cycle.
- Hover hints for crops, beehives, fermenters, smelters/kilns, cooking stations,
  already-picked wild resources, and tamed animals — remaining time, and a floating
  "!" once a beehive is full.
- Draggable skills block: level and progress bar for a chosen list of skills (including
  ones added by other mods), each removable with a small red "x".
- Draggable block showing your death count, carry weight, and armor value, each next to
  the same icon used for it elsewhere in the game.
- Pin icon on pieces and recipes, opening a draggable panel listing what you still need
  for everything you've pinned.
- Your own damage numbers show bigger, with a pop effect that grows even more on a big
  hit.
- "FedoHud" entry in the pause menu: toggle any of the above on/off, pick which skills
  to show, and reset every draggable block back to its default position.
